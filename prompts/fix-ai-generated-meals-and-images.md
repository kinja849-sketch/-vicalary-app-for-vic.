# Implementation Plan: AI-Generated Personalized Meals, Accurate AI Images, and Onboarding Synchronization

## Objective
Ensure that all meal suggestions and cookbook recipes are 100% generated by the AI based strictly on the user's onboarding responses (cooking prep time, dietary preferences, restrictions, liked foods, health goal) and local market accessibility (IP location), with accurate, dynamically generated AI food imagery matching each dish name, and calculated step-by-step instructions.

## Root Cause Analysis
1. `getCookbookSuggestions` in `lib/api/recipes.ts` bypassed AI and fetched generic recipes from TheMealDB/CocktailDB via `/api/search-recipes`.
2. Existing rows in `user_daily_meal_plans` cached old generic items (e.g. Avocado Toast, Caesar Salad) from previous Edamam scrapers, preventing new AI generation from taking effect.
3. Food images relied on generic stock collections instead of dynamic, dedicated AI image generation matching the exact dish title and ingredients.
4. Cooking steps were not enforced to be strictly within the user's onboarding prep time (e.g. 15–30 minutes).

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Dedicated AI Food Image Generation (`lib/services/FoodImageService.ts`)
- Implement a dynamic AI food imagery pipeline (using photorealistic AI image generation seeded by the exact dish title, ingredients, and cuisine style) so every generated meal has a unique, photorealistic photo perfectly matching its name.

### 2. Unify Daily Meals & Cookbook to AI Engine (`lib/api/recipes.ts`)
- Refactor `getCookbookSuggestions` and `getDailyMealSuggestions` to use the unified AI meal engine (`/api/daily-meal-plan`).
- Invalidate stale/legacy meal plan caches when generating new plans or when onboarding profile changes.

### 3. AI Culinary Engine Enhancement (`app/api/daily-meal-plan/route.ts`)
- Strictly read all `onboarding_responses` (`meal_prep_time`, `dietary_lifestyle`, `dietary_preference`, `restrictions`, `allergies`, `goal`, `weekly_budget`) + IP location.
- Enforce strict total cooking time (e.g. if user selects 15–30 min, total `prep_time_minutes + cook_time_minutes` must be <= 30 min).
- Generate realistic, locally accessible ingredients based on the user's city/country (e.g., Indonesia -> local market fresh ingredients).
- Auto-persist full ingredients array and step-by-step instructions into `cached_recipes`.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] All meals across Dashboard and Cookbook are generated by the AI matching the user's onboarding responses and local IP geography.
- [x] Preparation + cooking time strictly adheres to user's onboarding choice (e.g., 15–30 min max).
- [x] Every meal has an accurate, high-quality AI-generated image matching the dish name.
- [x] Clicking any meal displays complete, non-canned ingredients and well-calculated instructions.
