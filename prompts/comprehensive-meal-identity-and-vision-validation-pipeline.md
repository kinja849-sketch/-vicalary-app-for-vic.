# Implementation Plan: Comprehensive Meal Identity, Image Composition & Vision Validation Pipeline

## 1. Objectives & 4-Phase Architecture
Address all 15 architectural points across the 4 core phases:

### Phase 1: Database Consistency & Identity
1. **Schema Standardization**: Ensure `user_daily_meal_plans` strictly uses `plan_date` and unique constraint `(user_id, plan_date)`.
2. **Canonical PostgreSQL UUIDs**: Every recipe generated by the AI / backend is assigned an RFC4122 UUID (`crypto.randomUUID()`) used universally across `recipes`, `cached_recipes`, `user_recipe_interactions` (favorites), and `daily_meal_served`.
3. **Clean Favorites Binding**: `<FavoriteButton />` and `toggleFavoriteRecipe` operate strictly on canonical recipe UUIDs.

### Phase 2: Multi-Stage Safety & Candidate Assembly
1. **Clinical Safety Gate**: Programmatic hard-exclusion scan for allergies (seafood/shellfish and hidden derivatives like oyster sauce, shrimp paste, fish sauce, seafood stock), dietary lifestyles (Halal, Vegetarian, Vegan), and user dislikes.
2. **Normalized `meal_signature`**: Generate deterministic signatures (e.g. `indonesian-chicken-rice-grilled-rujak`) to prevent duplicated dishes across days.
3. **7-Day Repetition Rule**: Exclude previously served `meal_signature` from `daily_meal_served` unless explicitly favorited.
4. **12-Item Category Assembler**: Assemble exactly 12 validated, approved choices per category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, Drinks, Desserts).

### Phase 3: Individual Recipe-to-Image Binding & Composition Profiles
1. **One UUID = One Canonical Recipe = One Dedicated Image**: Image prompts and photography are bound to the specific recipe data (title, ingredients, instructions, cuisine), never inferred by array position.
2. **Diverse Composition Profiles**: Assign varied visual framing across the 12 selections in each category:
   - `top-down flat-lay presentation`
   - `45-degree restaurant angle`
   - `close-up detailed macro shot`
   - `rustic table setting with fresh herbs`
   - `minimalist modern plating`
   - `traditional authentic regional tableware`
   This eliminates visual monotony where multiple dishes share similar ingredients.

### Phase 4: Vision & Image Uniqueness Validation
1. **Zero Duplicate Images**: Enforce perceptual/URL uniqueness across all 72 daily dishes so no two cards on screen share an image URL.
2. **Allergen Visual Gate**: Validate that the resolved image does not depict prohibited allergens (e.g., seafood/crustaceans on a seafood-allergic user's meal).
3. **Structured Audit Log**:
   `[MealEngine] recipe_id -> recipe_name -> meal_signature -> composition_profile -> image_url -> image_status`
