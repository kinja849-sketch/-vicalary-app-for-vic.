# Implementation Prompt: AI Voice Assistant Intelligence, Voice Consistency, Latency, and Interruption

## Scope & Objective
Refine the AI Health Coach Voice Assistant to ensure:
1. **Dynamic Greeting & Voice Consistency**:
   - Eliminate hardcoded greeting strings (`Hi ${userName}! How can I help you...`).
   - Eliminate separate greeting playback routes (`speakText` / `/api/text-to-speech`) that produce mismatched voices or timing.
   - Dynamically generate greetings from user context (time of day, current profile goals, and recent activity) via the unified conversational pipeline.
   - Ensure the initial modal greeting, greeting on avatar selection, and all subsequent conversational answers share the exact same voice identity, speech model, pace (1.0x), and delivery tone.
2. **Intelligent Response Generation & Factuality**:
   - Directly address the user's specific questions using retrieved evidence and verified profile data.
   - Never invent facts, sources, corporate affiliations, or unverified claims.
   - Express uncertainty clearly and concisely when information is absent.
   - Prevent reading internal prompt instructions or exposure of private reasoning.
   - Skip search for routine greetings, profile reviews, or already-established context.
3. **Optimized Latency & Fast Turnaround**:
   - Respond immediately (< 1.5s) to greetings and simple questions.
   - Emit immediate spoken interim acknowledgement (*"Let me check that for you."*) for research queries in parallel with tool execution.
   - Stream verified speech to the user with zero dead round-trips.
4. **Instant Multi-State Interruption (Barge-in)**:
   - Allow user interruption during **both** generation (`thinking`, `searching`, `preparing`) and speech playback (`speaking`).
   - Cancel and invalidate superseded requests and streams via `AbortController` and turn-ID guarding.
   - Prevent assistant audio feedback/echo from misinterpreting speaker output as user interruption by comparing mic volume against current playback volume.
   - Discard late audio chunks from interrupted turns so old turns never resume speaking.
