# Implementation Plan: 12 Strictly-Tailored AI Meals per Category & Exact-Match Food Imagery

## Objective
Ensure that:
1. Every meal suggestion session (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) and Cookbook category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, Drinks, Desserts) contains **12 distinct, fully calculated meals** generated by the AI based strictly on the user's onboarding responses (cooking prep time, goal, dietary restrictions, local market).
2. Every meal title strictly matches its image:
   - Indonesian grilled chicken / satay shows authentic grilled chicken/satay (never a burger).
   - Desserts like Panna Cotta show authentic dessert compotes (never a savory egg/corn bowl).
   - Drinks show authentic beverages, and breakfasts show breakfast dishes.
3. Remove all generic hardcoded placeholder images and replace with accurate semantic culinary image resolution + AI photorealistic generation.
4. Eliminate hanging "Menyiapkan saran makanan..." states on Cookbook and Dashboard.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. `lib/services/FoodImageService.ts`
- Implement intelligent semantic culinary image classification:
  * Detects dish keywords (e.g., `panna cotta`, `ayam bakar`, `sate`, `soto`, `capcay`, `bubur`, `puding`, `jus`, `smoothie`, `gado-gado`, `omelet`, `ikan`, `tempe`, `tofu`, etc.).
  * Maps to verified, high-definition culinary photos matching the exact dish type.
  * Connects with photorealistic AI food styling generator for unique items.
  * Guaranteed category-aware fallback (dessert -> dessert photo, soup -> soup photo, chicken -> chicken photo).

### 2. `app/api/daily-meal-plan/route.ts`
- Expand generation output to **12 recipes per category** (12 Breakfast, 12 Lunch, 12 Dinner, 12 Snacks, 12 Drinks, 12 Desserts).
- Ensure all 12 items strictly adhere to the user's onboarding prep time limit (e.g., 15–30 min max).
- Match exact image URLs to the generated dish names.
- Batch persist all 72 generated recipes to database (`recipes` and `cached_recipes`).

### 3. `components/FoodCarousel.tsx`
- Expand `DEFAULT_MEALS` to 12 items per category with verified Indonesian & Asian cuisine photos (no burgers for chicken dishes).
- Update counter to reflect `1 / 12` items.
- Use `FoodImageService` for safe, category-aware image fallback on error.

### 4. `app/_pages/Cookbook.tsx` & `app/_pages/RecipeDetails.tsx`
- Update `CookbookCard` and `RecipeDetails` image `onError` handlers to use `FoodImageService` category-aware fallbacks instead of static generic URLs.
- Render all 12 items per category seamlessly.

## Verification Plan
1. Typecheck: `npx tsc --noEmit`.
2. Inspect `http://localhost:3000/dashboard`:
   - Verify active session shows 12 meals (`1 / 12`).
   - Verify "Nasi Merah Ayam Bakar" shows grilled chicken, not a burger.
3. Inspect `http://localhost:3000/cookbook`:
   - Verify each category (Sarapan, Makan Siang, Makan Malam, Camilan, Minuman, Pencuci Mulut) displays 12 tailored meals.
   - Verify clicking any meal (e.g. Panna Cotta or Soto) opens Recipe Details with exact matching image, full ingredients, and instructions.
